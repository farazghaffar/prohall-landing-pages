How to use

Your Select One routine in six steps

A thermal treatment: the product is applied, left in, then sealed with heat.

  1. Cleanse

    Wash hair twice with a clarifying or anti-residue shampoo. Towel dry until damp, not dripping.

  2. Apply

    Section hair into four parts. Apply evenly from roots to ends with a tint brush, strand by strand.

  3. Process

    Leave on for 30–60 minutes depending on hair thickness and texture. Do not rinse.

  4. Blow dry

    Blow dry hair completely on medium heat.

  5. Flat iron

    Flat iron each thin section at 450°F (230°C), 10–15 passes per strand.

  6. Wait

    Let hair rest for 48 hours before the first wash.

Before you start: always read and follow the directions on the product label. Do a strand and skin test 48 hours before full application. Use in a well-ventilated area and take care with hot tools. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, consult your doctor before using any chemical hair treatment.
